03 What’s inside

Everything a wedding needs.
Nothing that phones home.

Built to be read by humans, edited by hand and hosted on hardware you control.

01

Three themes

Classic, Modern and Garden. One NEXT_PUBLIC_THEME value swaps typography, colour and shape. Override the accent with a single hex.

02

Smart RSVP

Attending, headcount, meal and dietary notes. Zod-validated and idempotent — one answer per guest, updated not duplicated.

03

Guest admin

A password-protected dashboard with search, filters, live stats and CSV export. No spreadsheets to reconcile.

04

Logistics hub

Schedule timeline, venue map link and accommodation list — all driven by environment variables, no CMS required.

05

Registry & gifts

IBAN, Bizum or external gift links with a custom message. No payment processing, no card data, ever.

06

Five languages

English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. Each guest gets the site in the language their browser asks for.

07

Guest photo gallery

An invite-only gallery for the day itself. Guests upload from their phone, the browser resizes before sending, and photos publish straight to a shared grid.

08

Photos stay yours

Images live on your own disk — resized, stripped of EXIF/GPS and served only to verified guests. No third-party gallery, ads, watermarks or per-photo fees.

09

No lock-in

Export the guest list as CSV, keep the Postgres database and copy the photos off your disk. The code is source-available, so the site keeps running.

No analytics. No ad pixels. No guest data leaving your server.

Yours to keep

Guest answers live in your Postgres database, photos on your disk, behind your domain.

Nothing to leak

No third-party scripts, no embedded trackers, no vendor with a copy of the guest list.

Yours to leave

The code is source-available. Stop paying for hosting and it still runs.

04 Self-host

One evening.
A site for the rest of the engagement.

Clone the repo, fill in the couple’s details and start Docker. Postgres, migrations and the admin account come up with it.

  • Docker, or Node 20+ and Postgres
  • ~€3–5/month VPS and a domain
  • Vercel, Neon and Supabase also work — the photo gallery needs persistent disk

Self-hosting guide ↗ Environment reference ↗

terminal 
git clone https://github.com/minidevelopments-web/my-boda.git
cd my-boda
cp .env.example .env      # names, date, venue, passwords
docker compose up --build -d

→ http://localhost:3000
